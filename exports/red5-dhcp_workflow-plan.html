Red5-DHCP — workflow plan, one project

Continuous delivery of the same 67 panels and 4,467 points under one contract. Five phases overlap across nine months: P1 fabrication, P2 device network, P3 installation, P4 instruments, P5 programming. The completion date is the contract date plus nine months. DEJ is the prime contractor. TYBMS is subcontracted to DEJ. Furukawa Denki is not a party.

1 · Programme — one contract, nine months

C+0 is the contract month. The filled diamond is completion, the contract date plus nine months. The bars keep the durations from the 2026-09-05 plan. Phase N is the new device network, added because the installed Azbil cable may be IEIEJ p-A. The shaded band is the site window. 7 · Schedule tensions this workflow exposes

DEJ is prime, TYBMS is the subcontractor

Furukawa Denki has backed out and does not witness. DEJ submits the proposal and holds the main contract. TYBMS is subcontracted to DEJ for the BMS work: design, programming, installation supervision and commissioning. Stage gates that Furukawa would have signed are accepted by DEJ. Hotel operations still accept the plant back to automatic control.

One contract, same nine months

Fabrication, cabling, installation, instrumentation and programming are overlapping phases of one continuous delivery. The elapsed duration is still nine months. The completion date is the contract date plus nine months. Moving the contract date moves the whole programme. It does not lengthen it.

Programs still have to exist before the factory loads them

Bench loading still happens in months C+3 to C+5, and it still needs the application programs. Under one contract that is a sequence, not a second award: authoring starts in month C+1. If the programs are not ready, reduce the factory step to a harness test and load every program on site. That adds about an hour per panel to a night that has no spare hour.

One night-shift authority

Panel replacement, instruments and the program download still meet inside one night, 67 times. They are stages of the same contract now, so there is no second contract to wait for at 03:00. DEJ names the person who makes the 05:00 GO/NO-GO. TYBMS makes the technical recommendation.

The contract date sets the season, not the duration

Controller lead is still 12–16 weeks, and the purchase order still has to go out about a month after the contract date. The cutover is months C+6 and C+7. If the contract date is early October, those months are the April–May shoulder season. A later contract date keeps the nine months and lands the cutover later in the year, possibly in cooling season. The duration is not stretched to chase the shoulder season. The next comfortable slot after a missed shoulder season is about a year later, which means a later contract date, not a longer project.

IEIEJ p-A may force new device-network cabling

The 2002 trunk drawing says IPEV-S 0.9 mm². The installed Azbil cable may be IEIEJ p-A. That cable is not reused unless a test shows it meets the new network specification. New cabling is surveyed in month C+0, designed with the panel drawings, and pulled during the fabrication window so each wave lands on a segment that is already proved. Finding IEIEJ p-A on the cutover night is the failure this phase exists to prevent. It does not add a month.

Rate check the durations
